The quick brown fox studies language models.
A small corpus is enough for checking shapes.
We are not training yet; we are validating the data path.
Tokens become batches, batches become model inputs, and targets are shifted by one step.

This repository will later measure loss, perplexity, entropy, token preferences, and output distribution changes.
For now, the goal is simpler: load text, build a tokenizer, split data, sample causal language-model batches, and pass one batch through the LLaMA-style model.

Clear interfaces make later experiments easier to reproduce and easier to debug.
